ONE JOB, FOUR STEPS

From link to audio files.

01

Start the app

Unzip the download and run Start Spotify Downloader.command. The app opens in your browser and runs locally.

02

Connect Spotify

Sign in to read a playlist you own or collaborate on. The app uses titles and artists; Spotify does not provide the audio files.

03

Review the matches

The app searches YouTube for each song. Check uncertain results, pick a candidate, or paste the correct video link.

04

Choose MP3 or WAV

Download selected videos' audio into the Spotify folder beside the app. Progress resumes after you reopen it.

SETUP

Ready when you are.

You need Python 3.10 or newer and FFmpeg. The launcher installs a pinned version of yt-dlp from PyPI on first run.

With Homebrew on Macbrew install python ffmpegThen unzip and openStart Spotify Downloader.command

Know where the audio comes from.

Spotify supplies the playlist track list. The local app uses yt-dlp to find and download audio from the YouTube videos you select. YouTube availability and match quality vary. Download only audio you have permission to save. WAV conversion cannot restore detail lost in a compressed source.
