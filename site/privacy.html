with you.

The website provides the app download. The downloader itself runs on your computer.

What the local app handles

When you connect Spotify, it reads the playlists you choose and keeps the access token in memory while the app runs. It saves playlist tracks, YouTube match choices, and progress to Spotify/.state.json on your computer. It searches YouTube and downloads selected videos through yt-dlp. The MP3 or WAV files stay in your Spotify folder.

Data sent to services

Your browser and the app contact Spotify for sign-in and playlist data, and YouTube for searches and media. The website host may process standard request information to serve these pages and the app download. The website does not receive your Spotify token, playlist, or audio files.

Your choices

Close the local app to discard its in-memory Spotify token. Delete Spotify/.state.json to remove saved progress, or revoke the app in your Spotify account settings. For questions, contact support.ablais@gmail.com.
