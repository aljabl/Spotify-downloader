Privacy

The website provides the app download. The downloader runs on your computer.

Data stored locally

When you connect Spotify, the app keeps the access token in memory while it runs. It saves playlist tracks, YouTube match choices, and progress to Spotify/.state.json on your computer. MP3 and WAV files are saved in your Spotify folder.

Services contacted

Your browser and the app contact Spotify for sign-in and playlist data, and YouTube for searches and media. The website host may process standard request information to serve these pages and the download. The website does not receive your Spotify token, playlist, or audio files.

Removing data

Close the app to discard its in-memory Spotify token. Delete Spotify/.state.json to remove saved progress, or revoke the app in your Spotify account settings. For questions, contact support.ablais@gmail.com.
